# Phase 3 engine design review — Codex

1. **Blocker — §§2, 4.2, 5, 8. The two ranked profiles need different rules.** Tenhou explicitly disallows kokushi robbery of an ankan and includes the *whole* composite yakuman in pao. Mahjong Soul explicitly allows that robbery and limits liability to the named yakuman component. Both differences contradict the shared entries in `spec/rules.md`. Tenhou also allows formal tenpai on a fifth-copy wait when fewer than four copies are in the concealed hand, so the shared exhaustive-draw filter against hand plus melds is too strict for Tenhou. Split these policies and test each profile on the same fixtures. Clarify whether the Tenhou formal-tenpai exception also affects riichi eligibility before changing the line protocol. Pass the selected profile into typed legality and scoring helpers. The current `LegalRequest.actions` returns strings, and `Score.swift`/`Yaku.swift` bake in the default scoring behavior, including natural double yakuman. Preserve that default at the CLI boundary. Test the just-drawn/same-waits riichi ankan rule independently of the scoring profile. [Tenhou ranked rules](https://tenhou.net/man/#RULE), [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/).

2. **Blocker — §§4.1–4.2. Furiten depends on what happened, including when no decision was offered.** `rules.md` §4 items 27–28 require temporary furiten on a yaku-less completion, including when the seat has only pass and receives no prompt. On a surviving round, evaluate the offered tile against eligible shape waits independently of the prompt and whether the seat submitted a call. A seat with legal ron that chooses a call has declined ron regardless of call priority. If another seat's ron ends the round there is no subsequent furiten effect to observe. Apply the same missed-ron rule to eligible kan robbery. Track discard furiten separately over all actual waits, including called-away discards and yaku-less waits. Specify permanent furiten when a riichi player discards a winning self-draw, without disallowing future tsumo. For temporary furiten the draft inherits a conflict: `rules.md` says draw/call, while Mahjong Soul's ranked text names the next own draw. Prefer the documented draw boundary for Mahjong Soul and verify the interrupted cycle (own pon/chi, discard, another player's discard before own draw) against each client. Libriichi's `state/update.rs` explicitly marks yaku-less completions and defers marking a missed legal ron until the next event. [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/).

3. **Blocker — §§3–4.2, 6. Specify a kan transaction and draw provenance.** On kakan, expose the added tile for the rob window and retain the old pon until commit. If robbed, retain the pon, account for the winning tile once, and skip the kan count, wall replenishment, rinshan draw and new indicator. The uncompleted kakan preserves the winner's ippatsu. On unrobbed ankan, commit after any ruleset-specific rob window, clear ippatsu and first-turn eligibility, flip its indicator, then draw rinshan with the new dora active for tsumo. For daiminkan → replacement draw → another kan → discard, bind each pending indicator to its own kan. Mortal emits an older pending indicator before the next ankan, or after the next kakan's rob window and before its replacement draw. Mortal emits the post-open/added-kan indicator **before** the ensuing `dahai`, so ron on that discard sees it there. The draft's after-resolution ordering and default scoring differ. Settle ranked timing separately from the wire contract and fixture the chained-kan robbery as well. Mahjong Soul's published delay establishes the general order, but the exact scoring on a ronned discard remains unsure. Count the live-wall contraction once per committed kan and consume the replacement from the separate rinshan cursor. With one live tile before a kan, the rinshan leaves zero live tiles. Keep rinshan distinct from haitei, and preserve the origin of the ensuing discard for houtei. Under the supplied §2 definition, houtei follows the final **live** draw, with the final rinshan-discard ruling worth verifying in both clients. Zero remaining still gates calls and kans independently. [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/), [Tenhou ranked rules](https://tenhou.net/man/#RULE). Local reference: Mortal `arena/board.rs` (`step`), `state/update.rs`.

4. **Blocker — §§4.1–4.3. Give reaction and abort resolution an explicit precedence table.** Validate every answer against the same snapshot. Test ron over nonwinning calls, including chi/pon attempted on a riichi discard. For a ronned declaration, emit reach and dahai with no reach acceptance or charge. If a call takes a declaration discard without ron, accept and charge riichi before the call, then cancel ippatsu. Track double-riichi eligibility through declaration separately from acceptance. Resolve fourth riichi after the ron window and acceptance, preserving the fourth stick. After a fourth distributed kan, offer rinshan tsumo, then give the discard its ron window, then abort if no one wins. Current §4.1 would take a pon or chi first. Four kans by one seat permit ordinary continuation, with no fifth kan. Record the first discard per seat and separate table-wide interruption from seat-specific first draw/discard status. A shared boolean alone cannot represent four first-discard winds and chiihou eligibility after East discards. Nine terminals is an optional own-draw action that immediately produces its abort result. For four winds, explicitly decide precedence on the fourth wind against ron or a call, with ranked behavior **unsure**. Mortal aborts immediately at the fourth wind before collecting answers, so a fixture with a legal competing response is necessary. Define the same-discard tie rule if the resolver is generalized. In a valid four-copy wall, two seats cannot both hold two copies for pon, or both qualify for pon/daiminkan on that discard. Sources: `rules.md` §4 items 21–25, 37 and Mortal `arena/board.rs` (`step`, `check_four_wind`). [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/), [Tenhou ranked rules](https://tenhou.net/man/#RULE).

5. **Blocker — §5. End-game tests need a transition table keyed by outcome and dealer repeat.** The unconditional extension target check conflicts with Tenhou's published precedence for dealer repeat and Mahjong Soul's explicit double-ron exception when the dealer wins. Test an extension double ron where the nondealer crosses 30,000 and the dealer is the second winner. Define all-last for East and South games and distinguish completing the scheduled wind by rotation from repeating its final hand. Test the extension cap on a repeating final dealer. Evaluate bust on settled hand balances with 0 continuing, and threshold checks before awarding remaining sticks. Specify honba increments for repeats and all draws, reset on a rotating win, and apply the selected return points, oka, uma and rounding after raw-score placement. State both profiles' behavior for aborts and for a nagashi payout that drops a tenpai all-last dealer below the target or out of first. Post-payment tenpai-yame is a reasonable provisional default, with ranked confirmation still open. [Tenhou ranked rules](https://tenhou.net/man/#RULE), [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/).

6. **Should-fix — §§3–4.3, 6. Capture information lost by the hand-analysis types.** Keep the current draw separate from the concealed multiset and represent the discard choice (including the riichi discard) with origin. When a concealed tile equals the draw, both origins can be represented without globally unique tile IDs. Preserve the target, claimed tile and exact consumed tiles in round meld records. `Tile.swift`'s analysis `Meld` keeps only kind and tiles, and a kakan needs the old pon's tiles and provenance. After pon, the turn accepts only discards and forbids the called kind including the other red/plain variant. Reuse `Legal.swift`'s kuikae and dead-end checks and test equivalent red/plain waits and calls. For pao, record the triggering feeder on commit, identify which previously declared sets qualify (including an explicit ruling for prior ankan, **unsure**), and use the profile's liable component and honba payer. Nagashi should exclude any called-away discard, pay each qualifier without honba or stick transfer, and use the dealer's independent tenpai result for renchan. For double ron with existing sticks, compute per-winner deltas in order from the discarder, award the pool and honba once to the nearest winner, and allow the second winner to retain dealership. Consume the winning discard once in the physical ledger. Sources: `rules.md` §4 items 12–14, 21, 30–32 and `spec/formats.md`. [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/).

7. **Blocker — §§6–7. Specify Mortal's wire messages and the adapter handshake.** `start_kyoku` requires `bakaze`, `dora_marker`, one-based `kyoku` (1–4), `honba`, `kyotaku`, `oya`, four absolute-seat `scores` and four 13-tile `tehais`, followed by dealer `tsumo`. Fixed game seats must be distinct from current winds. Chi/pon/daiminkan need actor, target, pai and consumed tiles. Kakan needs the added pai and the three original pon tiles, ankan the four consumed tiles. `dora` carries `dora_marker`. Use `reach → dahai → reach_accepted` on acceptance, with acceptance before any surviving chi/pon/daiminkan or draw. Mortal can answer `reach` with a separate discard, so define adapter staging for the engine's atomic riichi action. Accept `none` as a pass or idle response and define what the subprocess must emit on every input line. Mortal's library `Bot.react` can return no response and accepts per-recipient `can_act` suppression. A `hora` has actor and target (equal for tsumo), with optional `deltas` and `ura_markers`; arena logs emit one per winner, including empty ura for nonriichi. `ryukyoku` has optional deltas, with zero deltas for arena aborts and settlement deltas for exhaustive draws. Reach charges belong to acceptance, and win deltas include awarded sticks. Terminate with `end_kyoku` then `end_game` as appropriate. Mask other hands and draws with `?`, and strip the seed and private metadata from live events because the seed can reveal the wall. Keep public kan tiles exact. Parser acceptance is plausible with this schema, but event-for-event action/scoring agreement is false as drafted. A Mahjong Soul ankan-rob event can deserialize without Mortal ever offering the ron. Exercise a pinned Mortal process and all four seat streams, including announcement ordering. Sources: Mortal `mjai/event.rs`, `mjai/bot.rs`, `state/update.rs`, `arena/board.rs`.

8. **Blocker — §§7–8. Outcome logs cannot reconstruct a complete engine execution by themselves.** Standard mjai lacks losing calls, declined actions, unseen wall tiles and unused ura. Mortal's optional two-word seed is not the Swift wall seed contract. An omniscient log can reveal the deals and observed draw streams, but a masked log cannot drive full-state validation. Use a versioned engine trace with initial wall (or portable seed plus monotonic hand ordinal), submitted decisions, and boundaries for exact self-play reproduction. For external logs, specify observed-tile injection or constraint-based reconstruction, infer effective passes where the sequence permits it, and report checks dependent on unavailable information. Distinguish live draws from replacements and infer both from kan transitions. With compound wins and draw reasons, compare terminal point changes and subsequent `start_kyoku` state as well as surviving actions. Keep identifying diagnostics with the first divergent event, seat and phase.

9. **Should-fix — §§8–9. Make the differential gate measure each reference's actual coverage.** Libriichi exposes `PlayerState.update` and `ActionCandidate`, discard masks, kan candidates, reaction validation and `agari_points(is_ron, ura)`. Compare coarse flags immediately on decision events and validate concrete candidates where the API distinguishes them. Sample before `hora` or an announcement resets candidate state or consumes chankan/ippatsu context. Compare base payments separately from the ledger. The arena runs whole games, including settlement and dealer progression, and can drive a reverse replay on an aligned subset. Its board differs from Tenhou ranked in consequential ways, including the four-winds short circuit and its multi-ron settlement path, which has no special triple-ron abort. It is an oracle for defined comparisons, with named expected divergences. Mercury can cross-check default-profile projections of hand legality and scoring. Its line protocol cannot check furiten transitions, priority, pao, multi-winner settlement or game flow. Add targeted walls and policy-guided players so rare branches are exercised, and report coverage alongside game count. Cheap invariants include conservation by tile kind and red status (exclude historical called-away pond entries, include dead-wall shifts), `sum(scores) + 1,000 × sticks` conservation, identical results under reaction-map order permutations, and equality of seat views when only hidden tiles are exchanged. Sources: Mortal `state/action.rs`, `state/agent_helper.rs`, `state/update.rs`, `arena/board.rs`, `arena/game.rs` and `spec/formats.md`.

10. **Should-fix — §§2–3, 6, 8. Specify ownership and benchmark the hot path.** Use validated public seat and wall construction, a single authoritative ledger, a separate hand ordinal incremented even on repeats, and canonical ordering of actions and results independent of dictionary iteration. Write down seed derivation and bounded random sampling with known wall vectors. Swift value semantics are appropriate, but retaining state snapshots and mutating nested arrays can trigger copy-on-write copies. Measure release-mode core games with and without event, legality and view materialization. At 500 hanchan/s, even eight hands of 60 draws imply 240,000 draw turns/s before reaction checks. The spike's 44,000 native `legal` requests/s includes CLI overhead, so establish an in-process baseline, profile typed action generation and reuse computed waits/decisions where warranted. Use a distinct masked event type at the player boundary. Define view fields needed for a standalone decision, including phase, current draw, source seat/tile, current winds, honba, sticks and public riichi status. A legal fallback must come from the current snapshot: after pon there is no tsumogiri. Separate malformed, illegal and missing replies in benchmark metrics. The existing WASI CLI spike leaves browser embedding untested. Keep process I/O outside the core, use a narrow UTF-8 buffer ABI with explicit ownership for eventual JS interop, and keep 64-bit seeds out of JavaScript Number. If keeping the Foundation-free JSON reader, test nested arrays, escaped names, unknown fields, bounds and malformed inputs.

11. **Suggestion — §10. Answers to the five open questions.**
    1. **Logs.** Start with scenarios and the scoped differential in finding 9. A practical permission-controlled source of human mjai is a small consented local or physical league where the project records all deals, draws and actions directly in mjai, using video or a recorder if needed, with participants granting fixture use. Tenhou's official manual restricts reuse and directs general mahjong users to contact it. Converted archives can be technically convenient, but the current converter's documentation expressly disclaims a license grant over its source logs. Use provider logs only with verified permission for the intended use. [Tenhou replay-use terms](https://tenhou.net/man/#AI), [Converter and dataset rights](https://github.com/NikkeTryHard/tenhou-to-mjai/blob/main/README.md).
    2. **Prompts.** Internally ask only seats with choices, while processing mandatory state effects for every seat. Give seats only their own prompt and keep eligibility and decision timing private at public boundaries.
    3. **Simultaneity.** Collect answers against a frozen snapshot and commit once. Keep deadlines and deterministic legal fallback in the adapter or scheduler. Independent reaction collection avoids exposing answers and avoids serial timeout accumulation.
    4. **Mercury.** Use it for hand-analysis projections. Spend the state-machine validation effort on independently written transition fixtures and a scoped reverse arena differential.
    5. **Unverified rulings.** Mahjong Soul's official ranked rules confirm closest-seat honba and sticks, so remove that uncertainty from the spec. Keep post-kan discard-ron dora and nagashi against tenpai-yame explicit and provisional pending controlled client fixtures. Fix their defaults before claiming ranked fidelity. [Mahjong Soul ranked rules](https://mahjongsoul.com/news/46/).

**Overall verdict:** Request changes before freezing the design. The pure transition API and separate seat view are a sound starting point. Resolve ranked-profile differences, specify reaction and kan commit order, and define the wire and replay contracts before the engine and independent checker proceed against the same specification. The two unresolved Mahjong Soul edge rulings can remain provisional during implementation with named fixtures and a clear limit on the ranked-fidelity claim.
