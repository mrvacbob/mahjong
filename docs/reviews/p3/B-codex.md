# Package B review (codex)

## Findings

1. **Blocker, reproduced. Tenhou offers an illegal ron after a declined tsumo.** [Round.swift:331](../../../Sources/Riichi/Round.swift#L331), [RoundLegal.swift:180](../../../Sources/Riichi/RoundLegal.swift#L180). Design §4.2 specifies temporary furiten on declining a self-drawn win for Tenhou. The helper only sets furiten in accepted riichi. In [tenhouSkippedTsumoThenAnkan](B-codex-repro/ReviewProbes.swift), seat 0 starts with `4444s56s678m234p8p`, draws `8p`, declines a legal tsumo by closing `4s`, and discards the replacement `7z`. Seat 1 discards `7s` before seat 0 has another live draw. Seat 0 is offered ron under both presets. The retained wait on `7s` and the unchanged pond with respect to that wait isolate the temporary-furiten rule. Add the §4.2 rules field with the stated preset defaults and set temporary furiten on declining a legal tsumo, including during a kan. Reuse the legal-win check for the pre-action hand and retain the flag through the replacement draw.

2. **Should-fix, reproduced. A robbed closed kan remains recorded as a completed meld.** [Round.swift:502](../../../Sources/Riichi/Round.swift#L502), [Round.swift:550](../../../Sources/Riichi/Round.swift#L550). [robbedClosedKanState](B-codex-repro/ReviewProbes.swift) drives the existing Kokushi wall and checks public state during the robbing window and after ron. Seat 0's meld list contains all four `1m` in both states, including the tile awarded as the win. The round reports zero completed kans, one indicator, no completed call, and a hand of only ten concealed tiles for seat 0. The phase and win say the kan was interrupted, while the same public meld representation used for committed kans reports it as present. Stage the closed kan as a pending transaction. Publish it as a meld only on commit. On robbery, leave the declarer's three unrobbed copies concealed and represent the fourth as the winning tile. Preserve the existing announcement and indicator timing. Extend the tile-conservation check in [SelfPlayTests.swift:237](../../../Tests/RiichiTests/SelfPlayTests.swift#L237) to cover the pending and robbed-closed cases explicitly.

3. **Should-fix, reproduced. Fourth open kan loses the owed dora event on an unronned discard.** [Round.swift:408](../../../Sources/Riichi/Round.swift#L408). In [fourth_open_kan_dora.scn](B-codex-repro/fourth_open_kan_dora.scn), seat 0 completes three closed kans and feeds seat 1's daiminkan. Seat 1 discards its replacement, seat 2 answers pon, and nobody rons. The round correctly aborts before making the pon. It emits `dahai(1), ryukyoku(suukaikan), end_kyoku`, omitting `dora(7z)`. Rules §1's `kan_dora_daiminkan_timing` says to flip after the discard passes without ron, and design §4.2 says to reveal the owed indicator at the next discard, after the window passes for Mahjong Soul. The author notes describe an extra abort exception. Under the written rule, reveal the owed indicator after rejecting the ron branch and before aborting on the fourth kan. If the abort exception is intentional, specify it in §4.2 and add this wall with the opposite event expectation. The engine behavior was reproduced. Client ordering at this specific abort was unverified.

4. **Suggestion. Separate win eligibility from complete scoring if profiling warrants it.** [RoundLegal.swift:97](../../../Sources/Riichi/RoundLegal.swift#L97), [RoundLegal.swift:180](../../../Sources/Riichi/RoundLegal.swift#L180), [Round.swift:631](../../../Sources/Riichi/Round.swift#L631). Both eligibility paths use full scoring, including bonus counts and payment/decomposition ranking, and settlement scores the hand again. This differs from design §4.4. Inspection confirms the duplicate work. I found no measured performance regression. Keep recognition in one scoring implementation and provide an eligibility path that stops at a valid yaku-bearing interpretation, or update §4.4 to reflect the measured implementation.

### Settlement, waits, and event payloads

- Pao scenarios exercised Daisangen and Daisuushii, liability from pon and daiminkan, tsumo, third-party ron, ron by the liable seat, composite yakuman under both presets, and a second ron winner. For the composite nondealer tsumo with one honba, Mahjong Soul charges the liable seat `32,000 + 300 + 8,000` and the other opponents `16,000` and `8,000`. Tenhou charges the liable seat `64,300`. On third-party ron, the corresponding split is `16,300 / 48,000` for Mahjong Soul and `32,300 / 32,000` for Tenhou. Both no-pao fourth-kan scenarios also ran.
- Double and triple ron scenarios checked ordered `hora` events and the nearest winner's sticks and honba, including when the dealer wins from a later position. Riichi declaration ron leaves the deposit unpaid. An accepted declaration transfers 1,000 from score to sticks before the next action. `SelfPlayTests.invariants` checks conservation as scores plus 1,000 times remaining sticks.
- Exhaustive settlement was exercised at 0 and 1 tenpai by `formal_tenpai_fifth_copy`, at 2 by [exhaustive_draw_two_tenpai.scn](B-codex-repro/exhaustive_draw_two_tenpai.scn), and at 3 and 4 by the named exhaustive scenarios. The 2-tenpai variant checked `[1500, 1500, -1500, -1500]`, two carried sticks, and dealer repeat in both presets. Nagashi covered sole winners, multiple winners, dealer nagashi, disqualification by a called discard, and dealer tenpai repeat independent of the payment.
- Own-discard furiten was exercised on a yaku-less wait and through a wait change. Temporary furiten covered an unoffered yaku-less discard, a pass, a call, a replacement draw, a passed added kan and clearing on the next live draw. Riichi furiten covered both passing a discard and skipping a self-draw. The new legal-riichi-ankan probe retains waits and checks permanence without relying on own-discard furiten.
- Inspected §6 round payloads against `Event.swift:21–47`, including sorted deal and call tiles, the three pon tiles on kakan, riichi-only ura, per-win deltas with scores updated in order, and tenpai flags at exhaustive draw. The scenario runner checks event text and result, while the replay test checks full event-value equality. JSON and masking are outside the requested files.
- Action order follows ordered seat/kind loops and sorted hands. `Set`-based tile deduplication sorts before use. Replay and suit rotation exercised deterministic output. I saw no unbounded quadratic path under the 14-tile/four-seat limits. The highest repeated cost appears in the bounded riichi-discard search and full scoring during eligibility. Hand-removal and settle force unwraps are guarded by action validation and a validated wall. No trap occurred in ordinary play. Initial scores, honba and sticks have caller-supplied integer ranges beyond normal gameplay. Arithmetic at extreme integer values was outside the verified play inputs.

## Disputed scenarios

1. **`closed_kan_rinshan_tsumo` — fix the wall.** All four opening tsumogiri are West, so step 6 reaches an ended `suufon_renda` round. The author's note about the stick is incorrect for this wall. Seat 0 declared on the first discard and had already paid. The reproduced result has one stick and deltas `[-1000, 0, 0, 0]`. The existing corrected-wall test replaces seat 3's draw and passes with 8 han, 60 fu and `ankan(0), dora(8m), tsumo(0)`.
2. **`riichi_furiten_skipped_tsumo_ankan` — fix the wall and the hand.** Four West discards abort before the claimed winning draw. With the fourth West replaced, the original hand permits tsumo but forbids ankan: `23444s` can read as `234s + 44s`, creating an `8p` shanpon alongside `1s/4s`. The ankan loses `8p`. The replacement hand `1112344s 123m 789p` retains structural `1s/4s` when it draws the fourth `1s` and kans it. Its `4s` wait is still physically available. The new probe checks that taking the kan makes the seat permanently furiten.
3. **`kan_one_live_tile_rinshan_not_haitei` — set next honba to 1.** Dealer seat 0's replacement win is rinshan and the dealer repeats. Design §5 step 2 adds one to the initial zero honba. Honba was the only mismatch.
4. **`last_tile_houtei_no_calls` — set next honba to 1.** Dealer seat 0 rons the last discard. The same dealer-win rule applies. Honba was the only mismatch.
5. **`riichi_ankan_waits` — correct yaku and payments.** After the kan, the winning reading is `123m 999p 33s 345s` plus the `6666s` kan. There are only two concealed sets for sanankou. Double riichi, menzen tsumo and rinshan give 4 han. Fu is `20 + 2 + 8 + 16 = 46`, rounded to 50. The nondealer mangan tsumo pays `2000/4000`. Including both riichi deposits and the winner's collection of both sticks, deltas are `[-5000, 9000, -2000, -2000]`. The existing corrected-expectation test passed.
6. **`nagashi_mangan_dealer` — expect `ryukyoku(exhaustive)`.** Design §4.2 expressly names the reason. Dealer seat 0 gets 4,000 from each opponent, keeps neither the carried stick nor the honba as payment, and rotates because the dealer is noten. Only the event label differed.
7. **`nagashi_mangan_multiple_winners` — expect `ryukyoku(exhaustive)`.** Each of seats 1 and 2 pays 2,000 into the other's mangan and receives a net 6,000. The dealer pays 8,000 and seat 3 pays 4,000. The stick stays and honba increments. Only the event label differed.
